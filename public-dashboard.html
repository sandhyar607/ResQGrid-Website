How ResQGrid helps you

Free public services during normal times and disasters.

🗺️ Area electricity map

See green, yellow and red indicators for every locality, search by name and check verified restoration times when available.

📣 Emergency announcements

Cyclone and flood warnings, planned interruptions and restoration updates — each with affected locations and instructions for citizens.

📝 Complaint registration & tracking

Report outages, fallen poles or exposed wires in under two minutes. Track progress with your reference number — no account needed.

🤖 AI public assistant

Ask questions in 12 Indian languages. The assistant answers only from verified portal data and never invents restoration times.
